# Frenzy Draught of Corruption & Immortal

### Overview

**Frenzy Draught of Corruption** and **Immortal: Frenzy Draught of Corruption** are combat consumables that provide offensive stat bonuses. The Immortal version retains its active effects even if the character dies.
