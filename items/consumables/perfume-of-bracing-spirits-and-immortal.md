# Perfume of Bracing Spirits & Immortal

### Overview

**Perfume of Bracing Spirits** and **Immortal: Perfume of Bracing Spirits** are combat consumables that provide powerful offensive and defensive bonuses. The Immortal version retains its active effects even if the character dies.
