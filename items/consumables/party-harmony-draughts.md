# Party Harmony Draughts

New **draught recipes** have been added to MoonBD, available in **x1**, **x10**, and **x100** crafting quantities.\
These new versions include both standard and **Immortal** variants, offering extended buffs and durations for different monster types.

***

### **Draught Categories and Recipes**

#### **Human Draughts**

* [\[Party\] Harmony Draught (Human)](https://moonbd.online/en/codex/item/?id=1401)
* [Immortal Human Draught](https://moonbd.online/en/codex/item/?id=1402)

#### **Demihuman Draughts**

* [\[Party\] Harmony Draught (Demihuman)](https://moonbd.online/en/codex/item/?id=1403)
* [Immortal Demihuman Draught](https://moonbd.online/en/codex/item/?id=1404)

#### **Kamasylvia Draughts**

* [\[Party\] Harmony Draught (Kamasylvia)](https://moonbd.online/en/codex/item/?id=1405)
* [Immortal Kamasylvia Draught](https://moonbd.online/en/codex/item/?id=1406)

***

### **Functionality**

* Each draught provides **temporary combat and life buffs** tailored for specific enemy types.
* The **Immortal** versions offer **enhanced effects** and **extended duration**.
* Compatible with new batch crafting recipes (**x1**, **x10**, and **x100**) for faster alchemy production.

***

### **How to Obtain**

* Can be created via **Alchemy (Processing → Manufacture)** once recipes are known.
* Ingredients follow standard draught composition and scale by crafting amount (x1/x10/x100).
* Check individual **Codex pages** for specific material requirements and production details.

***

These additions expand potion crafting versatility and reward alchemists with greater efficiency when preparing for specific grind zones or group activities.
