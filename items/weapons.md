# Weapons

## Blackstar Weapons

Players can exchange one [**Remnants of the Rift**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item//43786) at the [**Moon Master**](https://moonbd.online/codex/npc/900994) NPC in Velia to obtain a base version of a **Blackstar** weapon.
