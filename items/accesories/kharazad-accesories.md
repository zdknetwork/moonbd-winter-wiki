# 🟪 Kharazad Accesories

### Overview

**Kharazad Accessories** represent a powerful accessory line enhanced through **Essence of Dawn** and **Dawn Blackstone**.\
They surpass traditional accessory tiers in strength and serve as an advanced progression path.

### Crafting and Enhancement Materials

#### [Essence of Dawn](https://moonbd.online/codex/item?id=820979)

A pure essence imbued with dawn’s energy, used for **crafting** and **enhancing** Kharazad accessories.

* **Used For:**
  * Crafting Kharazad accessories
  * Enhancing from +0 to **NOV (IX)**
  * Creating **JIN Dawn Crystal – All AP**
* **How to Obtain:** Processing (**L**) – Heating Dawn’s Aura with eligible accessories.\
  Dawn’s Aura can be purchased from **Old Moon Manager NPCs**.

***

#### [Dawn Blackstone](https://moonbd.online/codex/item?id=820984)

An ultra‑pure black stone found near the Ancient Fissure of Mediah, radiating an immense magical energy.

* **Used For:** Enhancing **DEC (X)** Kharazad accessories
* **How to Obtain:** Dropped by **Dark Rift monsters**

***

### Notes
