# Exalted Soul Fragment

### Overview

The **Exalted Soul Fragment** is a specialized item used to further reform an already reformed **Blessed Alchemy Stone**, granting additional stats.

---

### Stats and Effects

Reforming an eligible **Blessed Alchemy Stone** with an **Exalted Soul Fragment** grants the following permanent stats:

* **All Damage Reduction** +2 (bringing the cumulative total to +4 Damage Reduction when combined with the Blessed reform)
