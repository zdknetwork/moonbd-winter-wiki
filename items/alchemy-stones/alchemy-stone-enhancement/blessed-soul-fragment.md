# Blessed Soul Fragment

### Overview

The **Blessed Soul Fragment** is a specialized item used to reform yellow-grade or higher **Alchemy Stones**, granting them additional stats.

---

### Stats and Effects

Reforming an eligible **Alchemy Stone** with a **Blessed Soul Fragment** grants the following permanent stats:

* **All Damage Reduction** +2
* **Monster Damage Reduction Rate** +1%
* **All Resistance** +2%
