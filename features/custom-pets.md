### Important Notes

* All Custom Pets can be purchased from the **Pet Shop**.
* Pet talents and special skills are active while the pet is owned.
* Multiple copies of the same pet provide cumulative bonuses.
* Additive bonuses increase existing stats directly and do not multiply other buffs or effects.
