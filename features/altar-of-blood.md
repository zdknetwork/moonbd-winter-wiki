# 🩸 Altar of Blood

### What Is Altar of Blood?

**Altar of Blood** is a **2–3 player cooperative PvE defense mode**.\
Players protect a central **relic** from incoming enemies that attack from four directions — front, left, right, and behind.\
The activity contains **10 progressively harder stages**, each introducing new enemy types, wave patterns, and boss mechanics.

The challenge rewards coordination, reaction speed, and efficient use of skills.\
Failing to protect the relic ends the run.

***

### Why Play Altar of Blood?

Players should attempt each stage at least once — not only for the challenge, but for the **unique rewards** provided through **daily quests**, **drops**, and **stage rewards**.

Each cleared stage can grant:

* **Heritage of the Forgotten** boxes (chance for Caphras Stones, Remnants of the Rift, or even enhanced TRI, TET, PEN gear)
* Daily repeatable quests from _Joe_ for useful crafting materials and upgrade items.

### Requirements and Entry

1. Travel to **Velia** and talk to the **Nightwing NPC** to start the introduction quest.
2. Complete the quest to obtain your first [**Nightmare Fragment**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item?id=296), required for entry.
3. Form a **party of 2–3 players** who have completed the same prerequisite questline.
4. The **party leader** uses **ESC → War → Altar of Blood** to initiate entry.
5. Entering a new stage costs:
   * **50 Energy** (party leader only)
   * **Nightmare Fragments** (consumed per stage initialization)

You can retry previously cleared stages using Nightmare Fragments only — no energy required.

***

### Preparation

Inside the **Altar of Blood**, **Maids** and **Tents** cannot be used.\
Before entering, make sure to:

* Bring a large supply of **HP/MP potions**.
* Carry **Medical Kits** — this is the only way to resurrect fallen allies.
* Note that **Elion’s Tears do not work** in this content.
* Configure your **Fairy** and **Pets** before joining (cannot adjust inside).
* Keep **Nightmare Fragments** on hand — essential to restart or skip already cleared stages.

All classes are viable, but **Witch/Wizard** are highly recommended for late stages (especially Stage 10) due to area coverage and survivability.

### Stage Breakdown and Mechanics

#### Stage 1 – Goblin Assault

* **Enemies:** Goblins attack from all four sides. They are slow and fragile.
* **Boss:** _Giath_
* **Tips:** Straightforward; focus on quickly clearing waves before they surround the relic.

***

#### Stage 2 – Orc Offensive

* **Enemies:** Orc warriors and **Orc Wizards** with ranged attacks.
* **Boss:** _Org_
* **Tips:** Prioritize the **ranged Orc Wizards** — unchecked they will damage the relic from afar and avoid grouping.\
  Clear melee orcs with AoE afterward.

***

#### Stage 3 – Skeleton Horde

* **Enemies:** Skeleton Wolves and Skeleton Soldiers.
* **Boss:** _Hexe Marie_
* **Mechanics:** Skeleton Wolves run directly at the relic in large numbers — bait them into clustering and burst them with AoE.\
  Afterwards, handle slower melee skeletons and Hexe’s curses.
* **Tips:** Basic positional awareness — a clean and easy stage if players stay grouped.

***

#### Stage 4 – Saunil Siege

* **Enemies:** Saunils and burst-damage **Imp Bombers** spawning from side lanes.
* **Boss:** _Saunil Siege Captain_
* **Mechanics:**
  * While main forces attack from the front, **Imp Bombers** gradually march from the sides.
  * If even one bomber reaches the relic, it deals huge AOE damage and may end the run.
* **Tips:** Assign one player to watch both sides; ranged players should focus bombers immediately.

***

#### Stage 5 – Imp Uprising

* **Enemies:** Imps and a mid-stage miniboss **Laytenn**.
* **Boss:** _Red Nose_
* **Mechanics:**
  * The miniboss Laytenn has high defense and will aggressively target players.
  * Kill him quickly before his pressure overlaps with the imp waves.
* **Tips:** Maintain aggro away from the relic. Red Nose is manageable after Laytenn goes down.

***

#### Stage 6 – Gatekeeper Gauntlet

* **Enemies:** Sequential waves of Nagas, Fogans, Crescents, then Cadries.
* **Boss:** _Cadry Gatekeeper_ (final wave)
* **Mechanics:**
  * Each faction sends a **Gatekeeper boss** with its wave — these can stack up if you don't burst them.
  * Each Gatekeeper prioritizes players but adds heavy pressure to the team.
* **Tips:** Focus Gatekeepers quickly to prevent overlap; use ultimate skills when multiple spawns align.

***

#### Stage 7 – Sulfur Blaze

* **Enemies:** Sulfur Mine mobs and **Lava Faoluns** (enhanced bombers).
* **Boss:** _Ferrid_
* **Mechanics:**
  * Lava Faoluns behave similar to Imp Bombers but move faster and are more durable.
  * If a Faolun reaches the relic, it explodes for catastrophic AOE damage.
* **Tips:**
  * Keep an eye on the mini-map for bombers.
  * Split coverage if necessary to intercept side rushers early.

***

#### Stage 8 – Basilisk and Centaur Siege

* **Enemies:** Snakes, Basilisks, and Centaurs.
* **Bosses:** _Basilisk_ and _Centaur Leader_ (two bosses).
* **Mechanics:**
  * Periodically, **Saunil Catapults** spawn and bombard the relic from long range.
  * Catapults must be destroyed immediately to avoid heavy damage.
  * Snakes are weak but fast; Basilisks are durable melee; Centaurs deal strong ranged burst attacks.
* **Tips:**
  * Destroy Catapults first.
  * Kill the **Centaur Boss** before the Basilisk — he deals deadly ranged nukes.

***

#### Stage 9 – Aakman Onslaught

* **Enemies:** Aakman soldiers and progressively larger **Kreators**.
* **Boss:** Giant _Kreator_.
* **Mechanics:**
  * Kreators appear mid-round in pairs, increasing in size and strength each spawn.
  * Their attacks **CC through protections** and can one-shot low-DP players.
* **Tips:**
  * Use range or mobility to evade Kreator shockwaves.
  * Coordinate CCs on Kreators to safely burst them.
  * Do not ignore smaller mobs — too many can overwhelm the relic.

***

#### Stage 10 – Final Stand (Hystria, Elten, Kutum & Laytenn)

* **Enemies:** Hystria mobs, three **Eltens**, **Kutum**, and finally **Laytenn**.
* **Mechanics Overview:**
  1. **Elten Mechanic:**
     * Kill all three Eltens within a short time window.
     * If one stays alive too long, the others revive with full health.
     * Time kills carefully and coordinate DPS.
  2. **Kutum Spawn:**
     * Periodically performs a suction AoE — avoid standing near the relic when he starts this.
  3. **Laytenn Boss:**
     * Spawns after Elten mechanic.
     * Has massive AoE swings and shockwaves capable of one-shotting even 380+ DP players.
     * Severe desync potential — keep distance.
* **Strategies:**
  * **Option 1 (Safe):** Ignore Laytenn initially, retreat to tunnel, and wait for Kutum’s periodic vulnerability after suction.\
    Burst him several times over \~10–15 minutes until Kutum dies.\
    Afterwards, safely engage Laytenn for a chance at a second **Heritage of the Forgotten Box**.
  * **Option 2 (Fast):** Fight Laytenn directly. Use a **Witch/Wizard** for Red Orb taunt to keep Laytenn distracted.\
    At around 40% HP, Laytenn kneels, giving a short safe DPS window.\
    Avoid getting hit at all — most attacks kill instantly.

***

### Tips for Success

* Bring enough consumables for multiple stage attempts; no resupply possible inside.
* Assign side-watchers on bomber stages (4 & 7).
* Prioritize ranged or siege spawns immediately (Catapults, Bombers).
* Save CCs and burst abilities for Gatekeepers and Boss intervals.
* Coordination beats raw gear score — communication is key.
