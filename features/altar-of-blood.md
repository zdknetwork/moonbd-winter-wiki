# Altar of Blood

### Overview

**Altar of Blood** is a cooperative PvE defense mode for two to three players. Participants protect a central relic from waves of enemies attacking from multiple directions. The challenge consists of ten progressively difficult stages, each introducing unique monsters, wave patterns, and boss mechanics.

Successfully defending the relic awards progression loot, whereas failing to protect the relic ends the instance run.

---

### Requirements and Entry

To participate in the **Altar of Blood**, players must fulfill the following requirements:

1. The character travels to **Velia** and speaks with the [**Nightwing**](https://moonbd.online/codex/?q=Nightwing&lim=50&t=npcs) NPC to start the introductory questline.
2. Completion of the questline provides the first [**Nightmare Fragment**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item//296), which is required for entry.
3. Players must form a party of two to three members who have all completed the prerequisite questline.
4. The party leader opens the menu and selects **War** followed by **Altar of Blood** to initiate entry.
5. Entering a stage consumes 50 Energy from the party leader and a **Nightmare Fragment** from each participant.

Previously cleared stages can be retried using **Nightmare Fragments** without requiring Energy.

---

### Preparation and Rules

Several restrictions and guidelines apply inside the instance:

* **Maids** and **Tents** are unavailable.
* Participants must purchase sufficient recovery potions and **Medical Kits** prior to entry, as **Medical Kits** are the only method to resurrect fallen party members.
* **Elion's Tears** do not function inside the arena.
* **Fairy** and **Pet** configurations must be set beforehand, as they cannot be adjusted inside.
* Carrying additional **Nightmare Fragments** is necessary to restart or skip cleared stages.

### Stage Breakdown and Mechanics

#### Stage 1 - Goblin Assault
* **Enemies:** Slower, low-durability Goblins attack from all sides.
* **Boss:** **Giath**
* **Strategy:** Waves are cleared quickly before they can surround the relic.

#### Stage 2 - Orc Offensive
* **Enemies:** Orc warriors and ranged **Orc Wizards**.
* **Boss:** **Org**
* **Strategy:** Ranged **Orc Wizards** are targeted first to prevent them from damaging the relic from afar.

#### Stage 3 - Skeleton Horde
* **Enemies:** Skeleton Wolves and Skeleton Soldiers.
* **Boss:** **Hexe Marie**
* **Strategy:** Skeletons are grouped together and defeated using area-of-effect skills. Slower melee units are handled afterward.

#### Stage 4 - Saunil Siege
* **Enemies:** Saunils and burst-damage **Imp Bombers** marching from the side lanes.
* **Boss:** **Saunil Siege Captain**
* **Strategy:** Designated players monitor the side lanes to intercept and defeat **Imp Bombers** before they reach the relic.

#### Stage 5 - Imp Uprising
* **Enemies:** Imps and a mid-stage **Laytenn** miniboss.
* **Boss:** **Red Nose**
* **Strategy:** The miniboss is defeated quickly to prevent overlapping pressure. Monster aggression is kept away from the relic.

#### Stage 6 - Gatekeeper Gauntlet
* **Enemies:** Sequential waves of Nagas, Fogans, Crescents, and Cadries.
* **Boss:** **Cadry Gatekeeper**
* **Strategy:** **Gatekeeper** bosses are focused down immediately to prevent multiple bosses from stacking up.

#### Stage 7 - Sulfur Blaze
* **Enemies:** Sulfur Mine monsters and fast-moving **Lava Faoluns**.
* **Boss:** **Ferrid**
* **Strategy:** Players monitor the mini-map to intercept fast-moving **Lava Faoluns** before they explode near the relic.

#### Stage 8 - Basilisk and Centaur Siege
* **Enemies:** Basilisks, Centaurs, and periodically spawning **Saunil Catapults**.
* **Bosses:** **Basilisk** and **Centaur Leader**
* **Strategy:** Ranged **Saunil Catapults** are destroyed immediately. The **Centaur Leader** is defeated before the **Basilisk** to mitigate ranged damage.

#### Stage 9 - Aakman Onslaught
* **Enemies:** Aakman soldiers and progressively larger **Kreators**.
* **Boss:** **Kreator**
* **Strategy:** Players use range and mobility to evade shockwaves. Crowd control abilities are coordinated to burst down the bosses safely.

#### Stage 10 - Final Stand
* **Enemies:** Hystria monsters, three **Eltens**, **Kutum**, and **Laytenn**.
* **Mechanics:**
  * **Elten Resurrection:** The three **Eltens** must be defeated within a short time window; otherwise, they revive with full health.
  * **Kutum AoE:** The boss periodically performs a suction attack that damages the area around the relic.
  * **Laytenn Boss:** Spawns with high-damage swings and shockwaves.
* **Strategy:** 
  * *Method A (Defensive):* The party retreats to the tunnel, waits for **Kutum** to finish the suction attack, and bursts the boss down over time. **Laytenn** is engaged afterward.
  * *Method B (Aggressive):* The party engages **Laytenn** directly. **Witch** or **Wizard** characters utilize taunt skills (e.g. Red Orb) to distract the boss.

### Rewards

Clearing stages in the **Altar of Blood** awards players with progression boxes (such as the **Heritage of the Forgotten**) and quest rewards. All drop lists, item rewards, and database tables are dynamically updated and available on the official [**MoonBD Codex**](https://moonbd.online/codex).
