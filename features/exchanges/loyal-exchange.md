# Loyal Exchange

### Overview

**Loyal** manages **Red Seal** and **Resplendent Medal of Honor** exchanges. These rewards focus on crystals, failstacks, exchange coupons, and rare utility items.

### How to Obtain

1. Talk to **Loyal** in Velia City.
2. Exchange Red Seals or Medals for rewards.
