# 🪁 Special Features

### Item Delete Hotkey

* Allows quick deletion of items using a custom key combination.
* **How to Use:**
  1. Open the inventory.
  2. Press **Delete** and **Left-click** on the item.
  3. Confirm deletion with **Enter** or **Space**.
* Designed for faster inventory management and cleanup.

***

### All-in-One NPC

* Combines multiple NPC services for faster access:
  * **Storage**
  * **Currency Exchange**
  * **Central Market**
  * **Skill Addons**
* All features are available through the **Moon Master NPC** in a single interface.
* Provides efficient access to essential systems without traveling between NPCs.

***

### Alchemy Tab Skip Button

* Added **Skip Button** for **Recharge**, **Polish**, and **Growth** actions in the **Alchemy** UI.
* Significantly speeds up repetitive alchemy processes.

***

### Stackable Crystals

* Certain **crystals** are now **stackable** to reduce inventory usage.
* **Known Bug:**
  * If multiple stackable crystals exist in the inventory, the system returns an error when attempting to equip them.
  * Only one crystal of that type must remain in the inventory to equip it.
  * Extra crystals should be stored in **Market Storage** or **City Storage**.
* This issue is **permanent and not fixable**.

***

### Merge Mails

#### Overview

* **Merge Mails** automatically combines multiple identical mails into a single entry.
* Helps reduce mail clutter and simplifies reward management.

#### Functionality

* Automatically merges mails containing the same item.
* Triggered once when the game launches via the **Launcher Play Button**.
* Operates silently in the background with no in-game UI indicator.

#### Requirements

* At least **two or more mails** must contain identical items for merging to occur.

***

### Farming and Crio Shop Updates

* All **seed drops** from farming are now **converted into boxes**.
* All **Crio Shop items** have been converted into **box-type items** and now include **white variants**.
* A new **processing recipe** allows players to use **Blue Seed Boxes** to create **Mysterious Seeds**.
  * **Mass Processing** is supported when using a **Processing Stone**.

***

### Moon Coin System

* **Moon Coin** is a special currency used for exchanging specific **Cash Shop items**.
* **Moon Coin Box (1000)** can be purchased in the **Cash Shop → Custom** section.
* **Central Market Cash Items** can be exchanged using **Moon Coin**.
* Ensures consistent and unified pricing for Cash Shop items.
* Simplifies adding new items without manual price adjustment.

#### Notes

* **Moon Coin** cannot be traded or listed on the market.
* Exchanges can be made at **Lux** in **Velia**.
