# 🪙 Gem of Fortune - Auto Buy

{% hint style="warning" %}
This item is not yet available.
{% endhint %}

The **Gem of Fortune** is a special item that grants access to additional Central Market functionalities.\
It is a permanent item once obtained and is tied directly to the **Auto Buy** feature.

***

**Functionality:**

* **Central Market Auto Buy Access:**\
  The Gem of Fortune is required to use the **Auto Buy** function in the Central Market.\
  Players without the Gem cannot use Auto Buy or benefit from any related features.
* **Tax Modifiers:**\
  Unlike its previous version, the Gem of Fortune **no longer increases tax bonuses**.\
  It simply enables Auto Buy at the system’s base tax rate.
* **Duration:**\
  The Gem of Fortune **does not expire** and remains active indefinitely once acquired.\
  If the system detects a missing or expired Gem status, it automatically refreshes or restores the buff.
* **Availability and Integration:**\
  The item can be obtained in-game and is fully supported by both the website and game client.\
  Descriptions and related UI text should reflect its updated permanent behavior and purpose.

#### How to obtain ?

You must either purchase it from Cash Shop or Exchange with Moon Coin
