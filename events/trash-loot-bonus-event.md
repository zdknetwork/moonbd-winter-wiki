# 🪙 Trash Loot Bonus Event

### Overview

The **Trash Loot Bonus Event** increases the amount of trash loot dropped by monsters across various regions.\
The bonus can range from **+25%** or higher, depending on the active event phase or region-specific adjustments.\
The system is designed to reward active grinding and applies to both **custom** and **high-level** zones.

***

### How It Works

* During the event, all eligible monsters drop **additional trash loot**.
* The **bonus amount** varies by event, usually starting at **+50%** and may go higher depending on event conditions.
* **Bonus trash loot** is not immediately visible in the character's inventory.
* Extra items are added automatically to the player's **Black Spirit’s Safe**.
* The reward appears after:
  * **Relogging**
  * **Character swapping**
  * Or returning to the **Character Select screen**

If certain monsters or items do not display the bonus properly, players can contact the staff with the **item name** or **Codex link** for review.

***

### Arsha Channel Bonus

The **Arsha channel (PvP channel)** permanently provides:

* **+25% Trash Loot Bonus**
* **+50% Item Drop Rate**

These permanent buffs stack with ongoing events.\
For example, if a **+25% Trash Loot Event** is active, Arsha Channel players will receive the event bonus **plus the permanent Arsha bonuses** for even greater returns.

***

### Coverage

* Applies to both **standard** and **custom grind zones**
* Focused primarily on **mid-high level** monster areas
* Incompatible items or exceptions can be reported via [Discord ](https://discord.gg/3xK6p7rhD4)tickets with the relevant **Codex link**

***

### Notes

* The **Black Spirit’s Safe** collects all bonus loot automatically; it is not possible to pick up the bonus manually.
* Bonus values may change between events or seasons.
* This event can run independently or stack with other **Loot Rate** or **Arsha Channel** bonuses.

Relogging after long grind sessions is recommended to claim all extra loot.
