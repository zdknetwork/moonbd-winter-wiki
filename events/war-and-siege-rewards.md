# War and Siege Rewards

### Overview

The **War and Siege Reward System** automatically distributes items to players who actively participate in Node Wars and Sieges. Rewards are sent directly to the in-game mailbox based on the guild's performance and the player's participation.

### Mechanics

* **Distribution Time:** The system evaluates participation and distributes rewards daily at 00:00 UTC for the previous day's events.
* **Active Participation Requirement:** To be eligible for rewards, a player must actively participate in the war or siege. The system verifies this by checking if the player has accumulated a score greater than zero in any event category (such as kills, deaths, building installations, or structure damage). Players with a score of zero across all categories will not receive rewards.
* **Reward Tiers:** The system categorizes rewards based on the type of event and the outcome:
  * **Siege:** Distributes specific rewards for the **Winner** guild and different rewards for the **Loser** guilds.
  * **Node War:** Depending on the specific war configuration, rewards are either split into **Winner** and **Loser** brackets, or distributed equally as **Participant** rewards to all active members of the involved guilds.

### How to Obtain

1. Join a guild participating in a Node War or Siege.
2. Actively participate in the event to accumulate an activity score.
3. Wait for the daily reset at 00:00 UTC.
4. Press `B` to open your in-game mailbox and claim the attached reward items.

### Usage Notes

* Inactive members who do not score any points during the event are automatically excluded from the reward distribution, even if their guild wins.
* Rewards are sent via in-game mail with titles such as "Siegewar Victory Rewards" or "Nodewar Participation Rewards".
* Ensure you have enough space in your inventory before attempting to claim mail that contains multiple items.
