# 🚩 Node War and Siege Rewards

### Reward Types

#### Node War

Node War rewards are participant-based. A player may receive a [**Node War Participation Box**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item//995590) for each eligible Node War attended.

#### Castle Siege

Castle Siege rewards depend on the guild's result.

* Winning guild members may receive a [**Siege Victory Box**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item//995592).
* Non-winning participating guild members may receive a [**Siege Participation Box**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item//995591).

Additional participation or performance-based rewards may also apply.
