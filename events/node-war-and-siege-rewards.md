# 🚩 Node War and Siege Rewards

### Overview

The **War and Siege Reward System** automatically sends reward boxes to guild members who participate in **Node Wars** and **Castle Sieges**. Rewards are delivered through in-game mail after eligible war records are processed.

### Reward Distribution

Reward distribution runs daily at **00:15 UTC** and processes the previous day's war and siege records.

Each war is evaluated separately. If a player participates in multiple Node Wars on the same day, each war can grant its own reward because each region is treated as a separate event.

### Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the configured reward rule for each reward box.

Some rewards may be sent to all recorded participants, including players with zero score. Other rewards may require a minimum total war score before they are sent.

Total score is calculated from recorded war activity categories such as deaths, kills, assists, siege structure damage, and other event score fields.

### Reward Types

#### Node War

Node War rewards are participant-based. A player may receive a **Node War Participation Box** for each eligible Node War attended.

* [**Node War Participation Box**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item/995590)

#### Castle Siege

Castle Siege rewards depend on guild result.

Winning guild members may receive:

* [**Siege Victory Box**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item/995592)

Non-winning participating guild members may receive:

* [**Siege Participation Box**](https://moonbd.online/codex/item/995591)

Additional participation or performance-based rewards may also apply.

### How to Obtain

1. Join a guild participating in a **Node War** or **Castle Siege**.
2. Participate in the event.
3. Wait for reward processing at **00:15 UTC**.
4. Open the in-game mailbox with `B`.
5. Claim attached reward items.

### Usage Notes

* Rewards are sent through in-game mail.
* Common mail titles include **Nodewar Rewards**, **Siegewar Victory Rewards**, and **Siegewar Participation Rewards**.
* A player can receive rewards from multiple separate wars on the same day.
* Reward boxes should be claimed only when enough inventory space is available.
