# 🛎️ Voting

### **Overview**

Players can support MoonBD by voting on partner sites once every 24 hours. This participation helps the project grow and reach a wider community. Each successful round of voting rewards **2 Mysterious Tokens** per day.

### **How to Participate**

The following steps outline how players can cast a vote and claim rewards:

1. The player logs in to the official **MoonBD Website**: [moonbd.online/account/profile](https://moonbd.online/account/profile).
2. The player locates and clicks the **Vote** button on the profile page.
3. A pop-up window displays two voting links:
   * **GTop100**
   * **ArenaTop100**
4. Clicking each button opens or copies the voting URL.
5. Completing the vote on both sites delivers the rewards.

### **Reward Summary**

Tokens are delivered automatically to the player's account upon successful completion of both votes.

| Item                 | Quantity | Frequency      |
| -------------------- | -------- | -------------- |
| **Mysterious Token** | 2        | Every 24 Hours |

### **Usage Notes**

* **Reset:** Voting resets every 24 hours. Players can check back daily to maximize token income.
* **Mysterious Shop:** Players can use [**Mysterious Tokens**](https://moonbd.online/codex/?q=Mysterious+Token&lim=25) on the official website [**Mysterious Shop**](https://moonbd.online/account/mysterious-shop) page to purchase exclusive items.
* **Interface:** The website interface includes buttons to copy links if the pop-up does not open automatically.
* **Verification:** Players must complete the captcha or verification on the partner sites to ensure the vote registers.
