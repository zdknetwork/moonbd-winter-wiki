# 🎮 First Time In Game

### **Overview**

This guide covers the essential steps for new players to take within their first 3 minutes on MoonBD. Following these steps ensures your character reaches 280+ AP immediately and has all server-side buffs active.

### **Step 1: Initial Setup and Rewards**

To initialize your character and claim starting items:

* **Main Questline:** Complete only the first few quests to initialize the account. Do not attempt the full main questline.
* **Black Spirit:** Open the **Black Spirit** (`/`) and accept all custom **\[MoonBD]** quests.
* **Inventory:** Open the starter boxes automatically placed in your inventory upon character creation.
* **Challenges:** Press `Y` to open the **Challenge Menu** and claim login and progression rewards.

### **Step 2: Use Redeem Codes**

Claim permanent rewards and supplies via the website. Visit the [Redeem Code Profile Page](https://moonbd.online/account/profile/) to enter these permanent codes:

* `MOONBD-WINTER-PERIDOT`
* `STARTER-MEAL`
* `MOONBD-3DAY-LIFEGEAR`
* `ENJOYMOONBD2026`

Check the [MoonBD Discord](https://discord.gg/3xK6p7rhD4) **#redeemcode** channel for the latest active limited-time codes.

### **Step 3: Obtain Starter Gear**

MoonBD provides immediate combat readiness through custom gear sets:

* **Automatic Delivery:** Check your inventory for a starter gear box.
* **Starter Quest:** Complete the **Starter Gear Quest** from the **Black Spirit**.
* **Power Spike:** Equipping this set grants **280+ AP**, allowing you to skip early-game grinding.

### **System Mechanics**

* **Moon Master Tome:** All **Adventure Book** rewards (AP/DP/HP) are integrated into this item.
* **Stat Bonuses:** Individual Adventure Book bonuses cannot be claimed manually. Any manual claims will reset upon relogging.
* **Custom Integration:** MoonBD integrates all essential rewards and stats through custom systems. You do not need to finish retail questlines.

### **Summary**

1. Complete the first few main quests for account initialization.
2. Redeem all permanent codes on the website profile page.
3. Accept all custom **Black Spirit** quests.
4. Open starter boxes and claim all `Y` menu rewards.
5. Equip your starter gear to reach **280+ AP**.
