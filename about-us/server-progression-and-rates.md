# 🔼 Server Progression and Rates

### Leveling and Experience

MoonBD features a streamlined leveling process to get players into end-game content quickly.

* **Soft Level Cap**: Set to Level **66**. Progression remains steady until this point, after which experience requirements increase.
* **Level 70 Path**: The server currently supports progression up to Level **70** for dedicated players.
* **Skill EXP**: Requirements for both character and guild skills are significantly lowered to ensure full combat readiness.

### Resource Limits

The server supports expanded caps for essential account resources to accommodate high-tier housing and worker networks.

* **Contribution Points**:
  * Soft Cap: 1,000 Points.
  * Hard Cap: 2,000 Points.
  * Maximum Limit: 5,000 Points.
* **Energy Recovery**:
  * All players recover **100 Energy** every **5 minutes**.
  * **MoonBD Premium** users recover **10 Energy every minute**.

### System Rules

Core mechanics are adjusted to maintain server stability and fair play.

* **PvP Access**: Players must reach **Level 64** to participate in open-world PvP and guild warfare.
* **Agris Fever**: A permanent **200% Agris Fever** bonus applies to all players for increased trash loot yields.
* **Character Deletion**: The timer for character removal is set to **64 seconds**.
* **Processing**: Processing mastery brackets align with the latest retail standards for high-yield crafting.

### Level Rewards

Reaching high-level milestones grants significant rewards through the **Challenge (Y)** menu.

* **Level 67**: Includes **Advice of Valks (+150)** and billions in **Gold Bars**.
* **Level 68**: Grants **Advice of Valks (+250)** and choices between **Essence of Dawn** or **Name Change Coupons**.
* **Level 69**: Provides **Advice of Valks (+300)** and **Inverted Garmoth's Hearts**.
* **Level 70**: The ultimate reward tier featuring **Moon Coin Boxes**, **Advice of Valks (+500)**, and exclusive pets like the **Baby Elephant** or **Hedgehog**.
