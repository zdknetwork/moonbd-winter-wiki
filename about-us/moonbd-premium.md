# 🎑 MoonBD Premium

### Overview

**MoonBD Premium** provides enhanced gameplay features, daily rewards, and strong character buffs.\
Available as **30‑day** and **90‑day** packages, it grants both in‑game boosts and exclusive web rewards.

***

### Premium Benefits

#### Premium Rewards

* Extra **Pearls** and **Loyalty** via the **Challenges Tab (Y)**
* **Daily Login Rewards** available in **Challenges Tab (Y)**
* **Premium Special Login Rewards** obtainable through the **website**

#### Premium Buff

* Max LT +2500
* Combat EXP +300%
* Lifeskill EXP +300%
* Mount EXP +100%
* Knowledge Gain +10%
* Karma Recovery +300%
* Luck +5
* Energy Recovery +3
* +100 Max Energy
* +30% Central Market Tax Bonus
* +100% Increase in Item Quantity
* +50% Gathering Drop Rate
* Additional **Teleport Scrolls** to Cities

***

### 30‑Day Premium Pack

**Item:** [MoonBD Premium (30 Days)](https://moonbd.online/codex/item?id=999998)

#### Bonuses

* (T9) Horse Emblem: **Dream Arduanatt (Pegasus)**
* **Tier 4 Fairy**
* **Advice of Valks (+150)** ×1

***

### 90‑Day Premium Pack

**Item:** [MoonBD Premium (90 Days)](https://moonbd.online/codex/item?id=900601)

#### Bonuses

* (T9) Horse Emblem: **Dream Diné (Unicorn)**
* **Tier 4 Fairy**
* **Advice of Valks (+350)** ×1
* **Advice of Valks (+250)** ×2

***

### Notes

* Premium login rewards are delivered through the in‑game **Challenges (Y)** tab and the MoonBD **website panel**.
* All buffs and benefits remain active for the entire Premium duration.
* The **90‑day package** includes all benefits from the **30‑day** pack plus additional bonus items.
