# 🎗️ Premium

### **Overview**

**MoonBD Premium** provides enhanced gameplay features, daily rewards, and character buffs. It is available as a 30-day or 90-day package. When purchased, players receive a box; opening this box activates the premium duration and grants immediate bonus items.

### **Functionality**

The Premium system applies account-wide benefits for the duration of the pack. Key features include:

* **Rewards:** Extra **Pearls** and **Loyalty** via the **Challenges Tab** (`Y`).
* **Logins:** Access to Daily Login Rewards (`Y`) and Special Login Rewards on the MoonBD website.
* **Convenience:** Additional **Teleport Scrolls** to major cities and a +30% **Central Market** tax bonus.

### **Package Rewards**

These items are delivered inside the Premium box upon purchase:

#### **30-Day Premium Pack**

* **Advice of Valks (+150)** x1
* **(T9) Horse Emblem: Dream Arduanatt**
* **Tier 4 Fairy**

#### **90-Day Premium Pack**

* **Advice of Valks (+350)** x1
* **Advice of Valks (+250)** x2
* **(T9) Horse Emblem: Dream Diné**
* **Tier 4 Fairy**

### **Usage Notes**

* **Activation:** Premium is delivered as a box item. Use the item to begin the 30 or 90-day countdown.
* **Website Rewards:** Specific premium rewards must be claimed manually via the MoonBD website panel after activation.
* **In-Game Rewards:** Check the **Challenges Tab** (`Y`) daily for additional **Pearls** and **Loyalties**.
* **Duration:** All benefits expire immediately once the timer reaches zero.
