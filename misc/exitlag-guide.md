# 📶 Exitlag! Guide

### Overview

**ExitLag** is a network optimization tool that improves connection stability and reduces ping.\
Follow the steps below to properly configure ExitLag for use with **MoonBD**.

***

### Step 1: Download and Install ExitLag

* Download ExitLag from the **official website** → [https://www.exitlag.com](https://www.exitlag.com)
* Install and launch the program, then create an account if needed.

***

### Step 2: Configure ExitLag

1. Open ExitLag.
2. In the search bar, type **"Black Desert Online"**.
   * **Do NOT** select any region labeled “JP” or “Launcher.”
3. Select **"Black Desert Online"** from the list.
4. Click the **Game Region** tab and choose a routing location.
   * Recommended: **DE Falkenstein** or **DE Frankfurt** (closest to the MoonBD server).
5. Click **Apply Routes** to save your settings.

***

### Step 3: Activate Connection Optimization

* Make sure the switch toggle for **ExitLag** is **ON**.
* Confirm that the **red dot** under the **Black Desert Online** icon is **red**, indicating the route is active.

***

### Step 4: Launch MoonBD

1. Start **MoonBD** normally.
2. When you load into the server selection screen, **ExitLag** should detect network activity.
   * You can confirm this when you see **traffic indicators** moving in ExitLag’s dashboard.

Once active, ExitLag will automatically optimize your network route for smoother gameplay and minimal packet loss.

***

### Notes

* Always start ExitLag **before launching MoonBD**.
* If you change servers or regions, recheck the routing settings.
* ExitLag offers a **free trial period** — no credit card required on the initial download.
